Hello! I'm Breck Meagher, an experimental physics PhD student at the Syracuse University Center for Gravitational Wave Astronomy and Astrophysics. My research focuses on developing technology for future gravitational wave detectors and ultra-high power laser systems.

ORCID LinkedIn GitHub

Projects

Ultra-High Power Photoneutralization Cavity

We're building an ultra-high power optical cavity for neutral beam injection in tokamak fusion reactors. I work on the cavity design, and our vacuum system.

Nonuniform Birefringence in GaAs/AlGaAs Optical Coatings

We mapped birefringence in AlGaAs optical coatings across the entire coating surface. I made improvements to the experiment design and worked out the mathematical analysis using Jones calculus.

Characterization of Defects on Crystalline Optical Coatings

I systematically studied AlGaAs optical coating surfaces using Scanning Election Microscopy in collaboration with the Stanford LIGO group.

Bathymetric LiDAR in Non-Ideal Aquatic Environments

We explored the attenuation and scattering characteristics of various visible spectrum lasers within simulated oceanic environments.
