I am a physics PhD student at Syracuse University, working with Professor Craig Cahillane on building a 1 MW photoneutralization optical cavity. My research focuses on the mechanical design of the cavity, including compensating for thermal effects. When I'm not at my desk designing or modeling, I spend time in the lab and machine shop making things work.

My background is in low thermal noise optical coatings, which has been helpful with building ultra-high-power optical cavities.
